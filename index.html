FROM YOUR FIRST REQUEST

Understand the process.
Know your next move.

Your application connects the information you provide with the offices reviewing it. Here’s how to keep it moving.

Read the application guide
  1. 01

    Prepare your information

    Have your student balance, guardian details, valid guardian ID with signature, and reason for assistance ready.

  2. 02

    Propose a payment arrangement

    Enter the amount you can pay and select an intended payment date within 30 days.

  3. 03

    Submit for review

    CSDL reviews your request first. Further review may involve Assessment, COO, or Branch Admin.

  4. 04

    Follow the decision and updates

    Check your status, respond to requested corrections, and follow the confirmed payment and promissory note instructions.

AFTER YOU SUBMIT

Stay informed.
Stay in control of your next step.

Your loan record keeps three different updates in view. Check each one when following up.

Application status

See whether your request is being reviewed, returned for corrections, endorsed, or approved.

Read the reviewing office’s remarks.

Payment status

Check how your payment is reflected. Keep your receipt or SIS reference ready if you need to ask Cashier.

A recorded payment does not confirm approval.

Promissory note status

Follow the PN tagging update separately from payment confirmation and review any instructions.

Contact Assessment if you need guidance.
