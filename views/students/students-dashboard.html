Student Loan Application Form
Form Description

This form is intended for PHINMA COC Students use only. Students can apply for an interest-free loan to ensure that examination permit is secured on time or request forward balance during enrollment.

Instructions:
  1. Application is open one week before every periodic examination within office hours: Monday - Friday: 7:30 AM - 5:00 PM; Saturday 8:00 AM - 12:00 NN
  2. Students must attach/submit one (1) valid ID of parent/guardian with signature.
  3. The maximum amount per student is Php 3,000.00. Loan in excess of Php 3,000.00 require the approval of the Controller or COO.
  4. Wait 1-2 days for the verification process. Status will be sent to your PHINMAEd Email Account or you will receive a call/text from CSDL/Finance/COOs Office.
  5. Approved loan amount shall be paid within 30 days.
PERSONAL DATA CONSENT

The information will be available to the school until such time the consent is revoked in writing.
