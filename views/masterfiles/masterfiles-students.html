Import Instructions (CSV formatting rules)
  1. Download the template — columns match export format for easy round-trip
  2. School Email (optional): if provided, use the local part ending in .coc (e.g. romeo-joi.santos.coc → becomes romeo-joi.santos.coc@phinmaed.com)
  3. Personal Email (optional): any valid email address, including @phinmaed.com.
  4. Both email columns may be blank. A dash (-) or N/A also counts as blank in the CSV. Blank emails leave existing addresses unchanged when updating a student.
  5. Contact Number (optional): accepts 09XXXXXXXXX, 9XXXXXXXXX, and country-code format 639XXXXXXXXX. A dash, N/A, or an incomplete number will not block the student import. Unusable numbers are left blank for new students and leave existing numbers unchanged.
  6. Date of Birth: YYYY-MM-DD recommended; M/D/YYYY (e.g. 5/15/2005) and Excel serial dates are also accepted
  7. Internal Scholarship (optional): enter one active internal scholarship name or code. An unmatched value is ignored and reported as a warning; the student still imports.
  8. External Scholarships (optional): enter active external scholarship names or codes separated by semicolons (for example, CHED; DOST). Unmatched values are ignored and reported without skipping the student.
  9. Family Details (optional): each name and valid mobile-number pair is imported; the family name does not have to match the student's name or surname. Incomplete or invalid family entries and old split-name or sibling columns are ignored without skipping the student. Existing family entries in other roles are kept when updating a student.
  10. New students are activated with an Awaiting Balance status until Assessment creates their balance
  11. An existing School ID updates that student's information; students with an existing balance remain approved
  12. Importing students does not send email notifications.
  13. Upload CSV (.csv). Review validation messages in the preview before importing
Import uses CSV (.csv) only. Maximum file size: 250 MB. Export supports CSV, Excel, Word, and PDF.
