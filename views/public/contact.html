QUICK ANSWERS

You might find
your answer here.

Guidance for the questions students ask along the way.

I registered, but I can’t sign in yet. What should I do?

CSDL needs to approve your registration first. Check Registration Status in Quick Help on the sign-in page. If you are approved but cannot remember your password, use Forgot Password.

My account is approved. Why is my balance missing?

Assessment prepares your balance record separately. Contact Assessment with your school ID number and school year so the office can check the setup.

Why isn’t my loan showing in Chat?

A loan conversation appears once a reviewing officer sends the first message. If no conversation is available yet, contact the office currently handling your request.

I already paid. Does that mean my loan is approved?

Payment verification and loan approval are separate. Cashier may record your payment during review, so continue checking your application status and the reviewing office’s instructions.

Where can I check my latest application status?

Sign in to your student account and open your loan records. Check notifications and any conversation linked to the application for follow-up instructions.
