STEP 01 / 04

Register your student information

Upload your school ID and complete your personal, academic, and contact details. CSDL will review your registration.

  • Use email addresses and a mobile number you can access.
  • Check your school ID number and academic details carefully.
  • Wait for registration approval before signing in.

STEP 02 / 04

Make sure your account is ready

After your registration is approved, sign in and check your balance. Assessment must prepare your balance record before you can apply.

  • Have your parent or guardian’s full name and contact details ready.
  • Prepare a clear valid guardian ID with a signature.
  • Check your dashboard for an available application period.

STEP 03 / 04

Complete your loan application

Open the loan form on your dashboard. Share your reason for assistance and the payment arrangement you can manage.

  • Enter the amount you can pay.
  • Select an intended payment date within 30 days.
  • Add the requested guardian information and ID, then review and submit.

GOOD TO KNOW

A few answers
before you begin.

Still have a question?
Does creating an account mean my loan is approved?

No. Registration approval gives you access to your account. A loan application is a separate request reviewed by the responsible offices.

Which ID should I prepare?

Registration asks for your school ID. The loan application asks for your parent or guardian’s valid ID with signature. Make sure the uploaded image is complete and readable.

How do I choose my payment date?

Choose your intended payment date within 30 days when filling out the loan form. Review the date shown in your application before submitting.

Why can’t I start an application yet?

Your registration must be approved, Assessment must set up your balance, and an application period must be available. Check your dashboard for the next step.

AVOID AN EXTRA ROUND OF CORRECTIONS

A final check
before you submit.

Review these details carefully so the office has the information it needs.

Use the correct IDs.

Upload your school ID for registration. For the loan application, use your parent or guardian’s valid ID with signature.

Keep documents readable.

Include the full document, readable details, and the required signature. Review the image before submitting it.

Check your payment plan.

Confirm the amount you can pay and your intended date. The date must be within 30 days when you fill out the form.

If your application is returned, read the remarks and update the requested details before resubmitting.
